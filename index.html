Scope & methodology

This dataset models China's relationships with Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan as a structured knowledge graph. Entities are typed and linked through controlled relations. Contested geopolitical assertions are modelled as Claims — distinct from neutral facts — with source citations. Mainstream entities (China, SCO, BRI) are submitted as stubs for match-and-merge with existing graph nodes.
